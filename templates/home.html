How the system works

  1. Modelled EV datasetdata/ev_market_dataset.csv
  2. Postgres in Docker, with a staged ETL and a star schemadb/schema.sql
  3. Sixteen analysis queries and ten viewsdb/analysis_queries.sql
  4. Tableau extracts the SQL layer and publishes three dashboards plus a story
  5. Flask embeds the published views and is deployed to Vercel
